## Changes made

Pages were read through the wiki API and converted into compact JSON; values were normalised (Identities at Uptie 4, E.G.O at Threadspin IV); effect sentences were partly converted into simulation rules and the rest kept as notes; some entries were merged, shortened or omitted. Errors introduced by the conversion are not the wiki's or Project Moon's.
