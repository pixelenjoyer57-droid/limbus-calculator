# Contributing

Sinner's Workshop is a **free, non-commercial, fan-made** project, not affiliated with Project Moon. There are no ads, donations or paid features.

## How to suggest or report

- **Discord** (ideas, questions, custom kits): https://discord.gg/hZgMnDN5K
- **GitHub issues**: https://github.com/pixelenjoyer57-droid/limbus-calculator/issues/new/choose (bug report, suggestion, rights holder request)
- Security problems: privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Terms

1. If you post an idea, bug report or custom kit, you allow the maintainers to use, adapt and include it in the app and its data without payment or credit obligation (credit on request where possible). You keep the rights to your own work.
2. Only share what you made or may share. No leaks, unreleased or datamined content, ripped game art or audio, or other people's private information.
3. **Code** contributions (pull requests) are licensed under the MIT license ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). You confirm you have the right to submit them.
4. **Game-data** changes (`public/gamedata/`) are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0 ([LICENSE-DATA.md](LICENSE-DATA.md)). The data files are generated by `scripts/gamedata/`; change the importer where possible.
5. Project Moon's material stays Project Moon's. Rights holders: use the "Rights holder request" issue form or contact a Discord moderator.

Be kind. Harassment, hate, NSFW content and spam are not tolerated.

## Development

See the README. Before a pull request, run `npx tsc --noEmit -p .`, `npx vitest run` and `npm run build`.
