# Changelog

Notable changes to Sinner's Workshop. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).

## Unreleased

- The About page's suggestion terms are split into short paragraphs; the suggestion form links to the contributing guide.
- Clearer terms for how suggestions are handled (About page, Contributing guide, suggestion form).
- **Easier to read:** better contrast in the light theme (the gold links, active menu item, selected tabs and the gold button and skip link) and for the small grey text on a selected list item in the dark theme. Selected tabs and list items now also show a mark besides colour. The light high-contrast theme now also applies when your device is set to light.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-24

First tagged release.

- **Builder** for Identities, E.G.O, enemies (including multi-part Abnormalities whose parts Stagger separately), encounters, choice events with Skill Checks, E.G.O Gifts, custom statuses, custom coins and coin types.
- **Rule builder** for effects with live game-style text. Rules run in the simulator, including triggering statuses, Coin Reuse and per-use limits.
- **Damage calculator**: thousands of runs per exchange, with best and worst cases that show every term of the damage formula.
- **Battle simulator**: a battle board with skill slots per unit, Clash odds, a battle log with every Clash round, auto-play, and batch runs for win rates.
- **Game data** from the Limbus Company Wiki: about 200 Identities (with sub-skills such as S1-2 and S3-2), 110+ E.G.O, 1,150+ enemy variants, 700+ E.G.O Gifts and 1,250+ statuses, ready to pick or copy.
- **Teams** of 12 Sinners and a Skill Check tester for choice events.
- **Saving**: autosave, Undo / Redo, workshops (save slots), session resume, backup files and share codes.
- **Accessibility**: dark, light and high-contrast themes, text up to 160%, Atkinson Hyperlegible, reduced motion, full keyboard and screen-reader support.
- **Privacy and security**: runs entirely in the browser with no accounts or analytics, a strict Content Security Policy, and safe limits on imported share codes and backups.
