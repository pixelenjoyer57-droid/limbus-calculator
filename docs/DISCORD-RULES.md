# Discord server text (paste-ready)

For the owner to pin in the server. Not legal advice.

## Welcome (#welcome)

Welcome to the Sinner's Workshop server. This is a small, friendly space for suggestions, questions and sharing custom Limbus Company kits made with the Sinner's Workshop app. The project is a free, **non-commercial fan project** and is **not affiliated with Project Moon**. No ads, donations or paid roles.

## Rules (#rules)

1. You must be **13 or older** (or your country's higher minimum age), as Discord's Terms require.
2. Be kind. No harassment, hate or bullying. No NSFW content.
3. **No leaks, ripped game assets or unreleased/datamined content.** Official art, audio and files do not belong here.
4. No personal information (yours or anyone else's). Do not ask members for it.
5. Use spoiler tags for story content.
6. No spam, self-promotion or selling anything.
7. Moderators may remove content and members. Disagree? Message a moderator politely.

## Suggestions and contributions (#suggestions)

- Ideas and custom kits you post here may be used in the app **without payment or credit obligation** (credit on request where possible). You keep the rights to your own work.
- Only post what you made or have the right to share.
- Code contributions go through GitHub under the **MIT license**; game-data changes under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. See CONTRIBUTING.md in the repository.

## Rights holders

If you own something shown here or in the app and want it changed or removed, message any moderator or use the "Rights holder request" form: https://github.com/pixelenjoyer57-droid/limbus-calculator/issues/new/choose . We will act promptly.

## Safety tip

Share codes and backups come from other people. **Import a stranger's share code into a spare workshop first**, not into your main one.

## Moderator checklist

- [ ] At least two moderators appointed, with a way to reach each other.
- [ ] Slow-mode (for example 30 seconds) on suggestion channels.
- [ ] A #report channel or a clearly named moderator contact for reports and rights-holder requests.
- [ ] Rules above pinned; verification level set to at least "low".
- [ ] Remove leaks and personal information at once; keep a short note of removals.
- [ ] Act on rights-holder requests quickly and record them (an issue in the repository is fine).
